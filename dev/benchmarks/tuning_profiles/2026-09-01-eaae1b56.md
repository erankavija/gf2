Each window's elapsed nanoseconds divided by its call count gives its sample.
The aggregate is the median. Spread is the nearest-rank interquartile range
divided by the median. The comparison band is the larger of the two spreads;
the selecting margin is the conservative median minus the asymptotic median,
divided by the conservative median. A win requires the margin to be strictly
greater than the band. This is a dispersion-based selection rule, not a
confidence interval or a probability estimate.

A lower-bound candidate is the first qualified asymptotic win and must remain
a qualified win at every later point. Earlier ties, losses, and uncertainty
failures do not prevent a candidate. A later failure makes a fully comparable
sweep non-monotone. Upper-bound selectors retain the preceding grid point when
the competing arm takes over. A fully comparable no-win or non-monotone sweep
retains its default. Missing, unreachable, malformed, noncanonical, zero-call,
zero-duration, or mismatched evidence makes a field uncalibrated and prevents
authoritative publication. The complete campaign has no such omission.

# ResearchGate full-text lead identification

## Lead and outcome

The lead advertised an author-uploaded public full text for the title
“Soft-decision decoding of linear block codes based on ordered statistics.”
The downloaded file is not the 1995 IEEE Transactions on Information Theory
article by Marc P.C. Fossorier and Shu Lin (DOI `10.1109/18.412683`). It is the
distinct 1999 IEICE work [Isaka1999] (`@/citation/Isaka1999`), whose longer
title begins with the same phrase. The lead is therefore a title-collision
false positive, and the 1995 article remains unobtained.

- Authors: “Motohiko Isaka, Robert H. Morelos-Zaragoza, Marc P.C. Fossorier,

- Venue statement: “presented at IEICE IT technical group meeting, Tokushima,
  Japan, May 1999.”
- Affiliations represented: The University of Tokyo, LSI Logic Corporation,

These title-page facts, the five-author list (four authors in addition to
Fossorier), the 1999 venue, and the multilevel-signaling scope distinguish the
file from the two-author 1995 IEEE article. The PDF itself is not redistributed
in the repository because this audit has no redistribution permission for the
local author-uploaded copy. For the audit's narrow identification question, the
pinned local-file hash and byte size, PDF metadata, longer title, five authors,
and dated venue statement suffice to identify [Isaka1999] and rule out the
advertised 1995 article. They are not used as a substitute for access to, or
content evidence from, the unobtained 1995 article.

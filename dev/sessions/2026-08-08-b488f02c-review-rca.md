# RCA — the b488f02c review ping-pong (2026-08-08)

Issue `b488f02c` (permanent-statistics feasibility study) closed through both
gates after **11 research-review runs (blocking-finding trajectory
6→4→7→8→5→7→5→5→4→1→0)** and **14 doc-review runs (5 failures)** spanning two
sessions, four measurement cycles (three full receipt regenerations plus one
equivalence-only extension), and roughly ten hours of wall clock. This note
explains why the loop ran that long, what the gate actually bought, and what to
change so the next study converges in a fraction of the rounds.

## Root causes of the churn

**RC1 — Generated receipts carry interpretive prose.** The harness compiles
narrative sentences into receipt preambles and per-row notes: censoring-bias
scope, watchdog causation, stream-base descriptions, "optimum" claims. Every
time review tightened a claim in the study, the same claim sat frozen inside a
committed receipt, and the contradiction was itself a finding. Because the
prose is compiled in, each fix changed `binary_sha256` and — under the
one-binary provenance contract — forced regenerating every receipt.
Regeneration cycles 2 and 3 trace entirely to this; the DEC-01 machinery
exists only because cycle 5 would have repeated it.

**RC2 — Provenance pinning without build discipline.** `binary_sha256`
self-hashes the running executable but nothing checks the executable was built
from the source SHA the receipt names. The session opened on the resulting
anomaly: receipts claiming harness `12bb1e3b` carried the pre-`12bb1e3b`
binary hash, and the committed throughput CSV held 50 of 105 cells from an
interrupted run. Cycle 1 (the first full regeneration) paid this debt.

**RC3 — No review convergence contract.** Each research-review round re-read
the entire artifact set at xhigh effort with no memory of what earlier rounds
had accepted, so silence in round N implied nothing for round N+1: the
blocking count *rose* twice (4→7, 5→8) as new lenses found new classes in old
text. The user-decisions clause (added mid-stream) is the first convergence
primitive; nothing yet scopes a re-review to the delta since the last verdict.

**RC4 — Two-binary/one-binary narrative residue.** After each provenance
decision, stale phrasings ("all five receipts, one binary") survived in the
study, the cargo-tree header, the handoff, and DEC-01 itself; three rounds
were spent sweeping residue one document per round. A single grep-driven sweep
per decision (candidate phrases enumerated, all linked docs in scope) would
have collapsed those rounds into one.

**RC5 — Hard-criterion literalism meets iterative wording.** REQ-01's
"empirically validated bias direction" clause failed the honest interim
wording ("argued, unvalidated") and forced deriving same-field validation —
which the receipts' own rows supported all along. The criterion was
satisfiable from day one; nobody enumerated its literal demands against the
artifact set before dispatching (the standing pre-dispatch criterion audit
would have caught both this and the missing generic-Ryser path).

## What the gate actually caught (the dogfooding win)

Roughly half the blocking findings were substantive, several critical:

- the stream-reuse defect behind the retracted q=5 anomaly, and the
  stale-binary/interrupted-grid provenance mess;
- the reversed HKS eq. 1.4 inequality, and later **C = 11 extracted from the
  theorem's proof** (two study revisions had called it unevaluated);
- the naive per-cell acceptance rule carrying a **79 % family-wise false-alarm
  probability**, replaced by a Bonferroni rule — which the very next sample
  vindicated (q=7 n=20 at z = −2.30, inside the predicted false-alarm class);
- REQ-01 coverage: the in-tree generic `permanent_ryser` path was never
  measured, plus the backend-enumeration second-list bug found while wiring it;
- sustained quarter rates computed by shard count while labeled as time
  quarters;
- the q=7 n=20 equivalence gap — closing it produced byte-identical GPU vs
  generic-Ryser permanents over 512 matrices at the study's most
  scrutinized cell;
- the determinant validation comparing finite-n data to the infinite-n limit,
  and the final anchor receipts with a byte-identical deterministic rerun.

The study is materially stronger for it. The cost was concentrated in RC1–RC4,
not in the substance.

## Recommendations

1. **Receipts emit data and mechanical provenance only.** Move every
   interpretive sentence (bias scope, causation, optimality, design rationale)
   out of generator strings into the study. A receipt should never be able to
   contradict a claim-strength correction. (Kills RC1 and most regenerations.)
2. **Build-state guard in the harness.** Embed the source SHA at build time;
   refuse to run — or stamp a loud `binary_source_mismatch` field — when the
   working tree or recorded SHA disagrees with the embedded one. (Kills RC2.)
3. **Convergence contract for AI reviews.** After the first full-scope pass,
   re-reviews scope to the delta (changed files/claims plus prior findings),
   and previously reviewed, unchanged text needs new evidence to block. Could
   live in the review prompts alongside the user-decisions clause. (Kills RC3.)
4. **Decision channel: keep and formalize.** `## Decisions` items (`DEC-NN`,
   user-authored only) plus the prompts' user-decisions clause ended the
   regeneration spiral and let review continue honestly (contradictions
   surface as advisory). Fold the clause into the profile package properly —
   see open item below.
5. **Post-decision sweep discipline.** Every provenance or scope decision
   triggers one immediate repo-wide sweep over all linked documents with an
   explicit candidate-phrase list, in the same commit as the decision.
6. **Mechanical pre-dispatch REQ audit.** Enumerate each hard criterion's
   literal nouns ("every applicable in-tree path", "empirically validated…")
   against the artifact plan before the first measurement.

## Open item — derived-state drift (needs a user decision)

`jit validate` currently fails with one finding: `contrib/gates/
doc-review-prompt.md` is `stale` — the user-decisions clause exists in the
live prompt (commit `333fda5b`) but not in the `sim-research` package that
materializes it. The obvious fix (edit the package, re-apply the profile) is
blocked and unsafe: the profile engine reports hard conflicts on re-apply, and
`gates.toml`'s hand-added `research-review` gate exists in **no** package, so
a successful re-apply would erase it. Hash-blessing the record was tried and
rejected — the engine verifies record↔package consistency, so there is no
sanctioned local-delta path. Candidate resolutions: (a) fork `sim-research`
properly — fold the clause *and* the research-review gate/prompt into the
package, bump the version, fix the re-apply conflicts, re-apply; (b) detach
the gate prompts from profile management; (c) accept the standing validate
finding. The peer JIT-audit session's `namespaces.type` example edit
(`type:simulation`) participates in the same conflict and should be resolved
in the same pass.

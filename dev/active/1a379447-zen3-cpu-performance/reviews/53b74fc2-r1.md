### Holistic findings
- `LaneMinSumRule` stands beside `gf2_coding::ldpc::MinSumRule` because the kernel crate does not depend on `gf2-coding`; the lane decoder task converts.
- The check step takes no mask: a terminated lane's inputs are frozen, so its output is unchanged.
- Variable-step cases hold at most one NaN operand per sum; the payload of a two-NaN sum depends on operand order.
- The four family producing closures were regenerated for the new source file.

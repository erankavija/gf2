## Holistic findings (Tier 3)

The seven blocking findings are not seven independent bugs. Five of them (F1,
F2, F4, F5, F6) share one shape: **the receipt records a number without binding
the conditions that make the number mean anything** — the host the sessions ran
on, the trials that were attempted, the plan the frames came from, the
independence unit the interval assumes, the pairing the comparison discards. The
protocol's stated contract is that a reader can recompute every decision from
committed evidence alone. Where that contract is broken it is broken in the same
way each time, which is why closing them one at a time will not work.

Two consequences reach outside this issue:

1. **F3 blocks `c077a88b` outright.** The LDPC survey needs runner-produced
   decoder cells with BER/FER intervals, and the runner structurally cannot
   produce an acceptable one today. That worker will hit this wall.
2. **F7 and F1 invalidate in-flight wave-2 receipts.** Any cold-cache cell is
   mislabeled, and any campaign resumed across sessions is unbound —
   `c7113c5a`'s accepted confirmation resumed over two sessions and flags
   116–119 of 240 samples in six cells. That second half is no longer a
   suspicion: the addendum below records the worker's per-arm recomputation
   proving the flagging is an artifact of F8's pooled threshold, which is why
   F8 is now blocking.

## Addendum — F8 is elevated from advisory to blocking

Recorded 2026-09-08, after the round-3 dispatch, on evidence from `c7113c5a`.

The reviewer classified F8 (`receipt.rs:1315`, outlier detection pools baseline
and candidate windows under one absolute median threshold) as advisory, with the
stated reason: "Current smoke receipts report no flagged windows, so this does
not overturn their stated outcomes." That premise is false. It held only for the
protocol's own smoke receipts, whose arms are close together. The first real
family receipt to run under the protocol overturns it.

`c7113c5a`'s confirmatory receipt
(`dev/bench_results/c7113c5a/2026-09-07-c7113c5a-polynomial-confirmation`)
reports ten cells. Six carry the outcome **Unstable**, each flagging 116–119 of
its 240 windows. The worker recomputed the flagging per arm instead of over the
pool and found **zero flagged windows in every arm of every cell**, with the
widest window anywhere at 1.21x its own arm's median. The pooled count is 0 in
all four cells whose arms lie within a factor of 7 of each other, and 116–119 in
all six whose arms differ by 167x or more.

The mechanism: `receipt.rs:1252`/`:1315` pool both arms' windows, `:1382` takes
one median over the pool, and `:1543` gates the cell outcome on that fraction
ahead of the decision. When the arms differ by more than
`flagged_window_factor`, the pooled median lands inside the slower arm's spread
and about half the windows flag by construction. `abtest::flagged_windows`
documents an *execution* median, so the implementation also does not match its
own stated definition.

This is not a sensitivity nuance. It changed the reported outcome of six cells
in a committed, accepted family receipt, and a re-run is deterministic and
cannot clear it. Under the protocol's own terms an outcome that a reader cannot
recompute from the evidence is a defect in the acceptance rule, so F8 is
**blocking** and belongs with F1–F7 in the round-3 required changes.

Two consequences for the rework:

1. Flagging is computed per arm, against that arm's own median, matching the
   documented definition. A fixture must cover the large-effect-size case:
   arms differing by two orders of magnitude, each internally stable, must
   produce zero flagged windows.
2. The amendment changes a published cell outcome, which reinforces the
   direction already given on F12 — this is a material change and the protocol
   becomes version 2.

`c7113c5a` also reports a naming hazard worth carrying into the protocol text:
its baseline is gf2 and its candidate is gf2x, so `improved` means the external
arm won and `fail` means it lost. Four `fail` cells are gf2 wins. A reader of
the scorecard cannot infer the direction without the addendum, and the epic's
final scorecard aggregates families with different baselines.

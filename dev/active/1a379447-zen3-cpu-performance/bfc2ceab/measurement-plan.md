## Existing families

The dispatch route inventory (jit:63bad95d, `dev/active/1a379447-zen3-cpu-performance/63bad95d/route-inventory.json`) lists 73 measurement families of other issues. 0 of them name a consumer of the inventory's entry points under the `family_scan` rule of [`inventory.json`](inventory.json), whose tokens stand for the prime fields, the sparse field matrix, the PLE, the permanents and the Gray-PAM demapper. Criterion benches name three of the consumers (`bench-sparse-matmat`, `bench-ple`, `bench-permanent`); a criterion bench is no protocol arm. The cost of the checks therefore has its own family.

## Family `caller-trusted-entry-check-cost`

The family measures a tree that holds every entry point in two forms: the baseline arm is built with no flag and publishes the entry points without their checks, and the candidate arm is the same arm package built with RUSTFLAGS `--cfg gf2_entry_checked`, under which the kernel crate's detection functions publish checked wrappers. That tree, the arm package, the plan projection and the launcher are the producing snapshot of each receipt (`inputs/producing` beside it); the plan records each arm's RUSTFLAGS and executable digest, and each execution reports the entry-point form its build publishes. The addendum's family description states the check each cell measures, the selection of the confirmation's cells and the decision rule; its `effect` object states the margins and their rationale. The sparse row cells measure the `per-row` grain of [`decision-table.md`](decision-table.md), the other cells the form the tree holds.

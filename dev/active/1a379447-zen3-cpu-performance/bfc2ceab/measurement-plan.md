# Measurement plan for checks that stay on a hot path

> **Diátaxis Type:** Explanation

Generated by `survey/make-inventory.py` from `survey/review.py`. Nothing here is frozen or queued; the plan states what a before/after measurement under the [measurement contract](../measurement-contract.md) needs once a check is chosen.

## Existing families

The dispatch route inventory (jit:63bad95d, `dev/active/1a379447-zen3-cpu-performance/63bad95d/route-inventory.json`) lists 73 measurement families. 0 of them name a consumer of the inventory's entry points under the `family_scan` rule of [`inventory.json`](inventory.json), whose tokens stand for the prime fields, the sparse field matrix, the PLE, the permanents and the Gray-PAM demapper. Criterion benches name three of the consumers (`bench-sparse-matmat`, `bench-ple`, `bench-permanent`); a criterion bench is no protocol arm. A before/after cell therefore needs one new family with one new arm executable, built once against the kernel crate without the check and once with it, each build pinned by content digest.

## Candidate cells

One family, non-regression objective, whole-consumer metric, single-core arm, warm cache. A first attempt admits at most six confirmatory cells (worker brief, P-20); the freeze recomputes the bound from the family's ledger.

| Cell | Interface | Workload | Why this cell |
|---|---|---|---|
| `spmm-small-narrow` | `small-prime-spmm-row` | `SparseFieldMatrix::matmat` over a prime at most 251, dense operand of 16 columns | whole consumer; the scan is largest against the kernel's work when the row has one 16-column block |
| `spmm-small-wide` | `small-prime-spmm-row` | `SparseFieldMatrix::matmat` over a prime at most 251, dense operand of 1024 columns | whole consumer; the scan is amortized over 64 blocks per row |
| `spmm-medium-narrow` | `medium-prime-spmm-row` | `SparseFieldMatrix::matmat` over a prime above 251, dense operand of 16 columns | whole consumer; the u16 lane |
| `permanent-f3-singleword-simd` | `bipedal-binary` | `permanent_bipedal3_singleword_simd` on one matrix | whole consumer; one checked kernel call per Gray step |
| `ple-small-panel` | `small-prime-ple-panel` | `FieldMatrix::ple` over a prime at most 251 at a width the panel base takes | whole consumer; one bounds check per pivot |
| `matvec-small-packed` | `small-prime-gemm-row-panel` | the packed small-prime matrix-vector product | whole consumer; one checked product per call, as the control of the per-call class |

The interfaces without a cell run their check once per matrix product or per demapped axis, or are called from no tracked Rust file; [`decision-table.md`](decision-table.md) lists each.

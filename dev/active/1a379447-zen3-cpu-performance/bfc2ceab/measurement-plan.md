## Existing families

The dispatch route inventory (jit:63bad95d, `dev/active/1a379447-zen3-cpu-performance/63bad95d/route-inventory.json`) lists 73 measurement families. 0 of them name a consumer of the inventory's entry points under the `family_scan` rule of [`inventory.json`](inventory.json), whose tokens stand for the prime fields, the sparse field matrix, the PLE, the permanents and the Gray-PAM demapper. Criterion benches name three of the consumers (`bench-sparse-matmat`, `bench-ple`, `bench-permanent`); a criterion bench is no protocol arm. The cost of the checks therefore has its own family.

## Family `caller-trusted-entry-check-cost`

Both arms are the arm package `survey/entry-check-arm` built twice from one tree: the baseline with no flag and the candidate with RUSTFLAGS `--cfg gf2_entry_checked`, under which the kernel crate's detection functions publish the checked wrappers. The plan records each arm's RUSTFLAGS and executable digest, and each execution reports the entry-point form its build publishes, observed from the linked kernel crate. The addendum's family description states the check each cell measures, the selection of the confirmation's cells and the decision rule; its `effect` object states the margins and their rationale.

## Stages

- Untimed: `survey/smoke-arms.sh pilot` drives both arms of every cell through `benchmark-ab-runner smoke` and writes [`survey/pilot-smoke.json`](survey/pilot-smoke.json).
- Pilot: the queue line `dev/bench_results/bfc2ceab/run-entry-check.sh window pilot`, every cell exploratory.
- Confirmation: `run-entry-check.sh freeze` derives the confirmation addendum from the committed pilot receipt with the canonical freezer; a first confirmatory attempt spends half the family alpha, and P-20's tail-support bound then admits six confirmatory cells.

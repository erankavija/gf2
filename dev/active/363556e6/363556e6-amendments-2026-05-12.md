## Amendment summary

The original S3 criteria assumed concurrent access to both an AVX2-only and an
AVX-512-capable host. The dev host is AMD Ryzen 9 5900X (Zen 3), which has AVX2
only; no AVX-512 hardware was available for this issue's measurement window.

The user direction is to **scope S3 down to AVX2-only on the dev host**, and to
**defer the AVX-512 throughput continuation** to a dedicated follow-up issue
under the SIMD-and-platform-expansion epic.

The dedicated follow-up issue was filed on 2026-05-12 as `f8d230ef`
("AVX-512 zmm bipedal-3 kernel for permanent_bipedal3"), wired as a dependency
of `7f809931` (the SIMD-and-platform-expansion epic). f8d230ef's success
criteria include the AVX-512 vs AVX2 throughput ratio measurement that S3
originally tried to cover.

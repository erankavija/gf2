## Scatter share of the BICM channel across sessions

The share is formed inside each session: that session's direct-scatter statistic divided by its own full max-log BICM channel statistic for the same MODCOD. The rows are the median of those per-session shares with the order-statistic interval at coverage 0.961. The two cases run as separate processes under the counting allocator, so the share describes composition and decides nothing; the materiality decision is the paired receipt's. The Amdahl ceiling is an **estimate**: it is 1/(1 - share), the single-worker speedup that would follow if the scatter vanished at no replacement cost and nothing else changed, and a measured speedup below it refutes nothing.

# Mid-range buffer evidence synthesis

> **Diátaxis Type:** Explanation

Story `2037941f` profiles 8-64-word logical-buffer operations and their dense consumers on the Ryzen 9 5900X and selects no production route change. The [generated tables](mid-range-tables.md) carry the interval, decision, outcome and sample count of every campaign cell with each campaign's receipt and executable digests, host and toolchain; profile figures stay in the linked profile summaries; `python3 -B dev/active/2037941f-profile-and-optimize-mid-range-buffer-operations/survey/make-mid-range-tables.py` regenerates them from committed bytes. This document interprets those rows against the story criteria and states no figure of its own. The external comparators are ISA-L [IsaL2026] and M4RI [AlbrechtBard2026].

## Isolated throughput and measured consumer benefit

The Metric column of every [cell table](mid-range-tables.md#cells) is the frozen addendum's cost boundary. `kernel-isolated` cells are the isolated XOR, public row-XOR, ISA-L scalar-gap and isolated fused-parity families; `whole-consumer` cells are the NR constructor, the allocated public `matvec` and the M4RI comparator.

- **Isolated throughput.** The unroll pilots are the only candidate measurements of the story. Their isolated-XOR rows record `not-worse` or `inconclusive` at every cell; they are exploratory and establish no isolated gain under the frozen worthwhile-effect margin.
- **Consumer-side condition.** The logical addendum admits a kernel only when every matching public row-XOR anchor is `improved`. The public row-XOR rows of both factors record `regressed`, `inconclusive` or `not-worse`, so an isolated result of either factor stays an isolated result.
- **Whole consumers.** No whole-consumer cell compares a candidate with the established route. The NR cells and the identity cells of the allocated `matvec` campaign compare one executable with itself and measure dispersion. Its `scalar-reference` cells compare the selected lane with a reference build the addendum admits for attribution only, and the M4RI cells compare an external library and attribute a gap. The story therefore measures no consumer benefit, and claims none.
- **Comparator direction.** In every ISA-L and M4RI cell the compared arm is the external library and the decision is `regressed`: the external arm is slower than its gf2 peer at that cell's boundary. The ISA-L rows hold for the scalar `xor_gen_base` arm, with the gf2 copy and the ISA-L pointer array inside the timed call as the [comparison record](isal-base-gap-results.md) states. The M4RI rows hold for the fresh whole-consumer boundary and for the retained-state boundary, which still creates, unpacks and disposes the M4RI output. Neither family measures isolated `mzd_mul` or dispatched `xor_gen` throughput.

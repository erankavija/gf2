# Corrections to the frozen dense-parity instruments

> **Diátaxis Type:** Reference

The [dense-parity addendum](dense-parity-addendum.md), its superseded
[version 1](dense-parity-addendum-v1.md) and the
[dense-parity harness description](dense-parity-harness.md) are producing
inputs the dense-parity receipts pin by digest, and keep their bytes. This
record quotes each statement in them about the tree their source citations
describe, states what the committed evidence establishes, and gives the reading
the other documents of the story use.

No correction changes a cell, a margin, a limit, a decision rule or a source
claim.

## C-01: the tree the addendum's source claims describe

Both addendum versions, opening section:

> Every claim this document makes about current gf2 code is a claim of
> [`survey/dense-parity-source-evidence.json`](survey/dense-parity-source-evidence.json),
> regenerated by
> [`survey/make-dense-parity-source-evidence.py`](survey/make-dense-parity-source-evidence.py),
> which fails when a pinned fragment disappears or changes its occurrence
> count and records the line each fragment currently occupies.

**Narrowed.** The committed ledger records, for each claim, a commit and the
digest of the cited file at that commit. Each claim holds there, and the
[verification record](../1a379447-zen3-cpu-performance/6833c5b5/dense-parity-source-evidence-verification.json)
lists the claims whose file or line differs in the revision it compares.

**Reading used.** "Current gf2 code" is the cited file at the commit its ledger
row records. A source claim of either addendum, including each "source
evidence" reference in its body, describes that file and not the working tree.
The [verification](../1a379447-zen3-cpu-performance/6833c5b5/verification.md)
gives the check.

**Decisions.** Unaffected: each receipt pins its producing sources per file, so
no cell, margin or verdict reads the ledger's line positions.

## C-02: regeneration of the ledger

Harness description, "Provenance artifacts":

> `survey/make-dense-parity-source-evidence.py` writes
> `survey/dense-parity-source-evidence.json`, where every source claim the frozen
> addendum makes records its project, commit, path, line, the verbatim line and
> why. Both are regenerated rather than edited, so a claim that moves fails its
> generator instead of going stale in prose.

**Narrowed.** The generator reads the working tree and fails when a fragment
disappears or changes its occurrence count; a fragment that moves is written at
its new line. The committed ledger keeps the bytes the receipts pin and
describes the files at its recorded commits, as C-01 states.

**Reading used.** The ledger is a record of its recorded commits, verified
against their content. The closure manifest the same paragraph names is
regenerated and checked against the working tree by
`survey/make-dense-producing-inputs.py --check`.

**Decisions.** Unaffected, for the reason C-01 gives.

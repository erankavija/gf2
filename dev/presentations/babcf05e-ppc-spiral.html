Where the spiral said "stop"

Dense multithread

Mandatory pre-flight rejected V10 row-tile rayon for dense transpose and M4RM.

Cache miss ratios: 2.0071% for transpose/4096 and 1.4102% for m4rm_1024x1024, both above the 1% threshold.

Sparse layout

Block-CSR, RCM, and prefetch scaffolds landed with honest negative/limited benchmark evidence.

Kept as infrastructure and measurement foundation rather than overstated speedup claims.

Future work

  • Design a lower-overhead GF(2) Strassen/Winograd path with view-native M4RM leaves before enabling any production crossover.
  • Extend the fflas reference harness to rectangular GF(p) cells and schedule slow 4096^3 sweeps outside the agent fast lane.
  • Attack small-prime GF(p) GEMM parity, especially GF(7) and GF(251), where fflas remains far ahead.
  • Batch multiple GF(2^m) FieldMatrix output cells per panel to reduce dot setup/export overhead.
  • Revisit sparse matvec layout after workload-specific matrix corpora and hardware-counter-guided blocking choices are available.
  • Consider GPU offload only after CPU paths are compute-bound and reuse is high enough to amortize transfer costs.

Takeaway

The epic delivered measurable speedups where the PPC spiral found compute-bound work, and it deliberately avoided production dispatch where measurement showed slower or memory-bound paths.

That leaves gf2-core faster, better instrumented, and clearer about the next research-grade performance problems.

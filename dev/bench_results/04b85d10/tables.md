Seeds and generators. The fixture seed is the addendum cell's `workload.seed`: `gf2_core` `BitVec::random_seeded` and `BitMatrix::random_seeded` turn it, and the per-row, per-block and per-bank offsets of it that `prepare` defines, into fixture words through `rand::rngs::StdRng::seed_from_u64` and `Rng::fill`. The generator is ChaCha12, whose 32-byte key `seed_from_u64` expands from the seed with PCG32, pinned by the crate versions each receipt's snapshotted harness lockfile resolves. Its derivation is `dev/active/04b85d10/survey/gf2-side/src/lib.rs`. A seed marked unused belongs to a deterministic fixture that draws nothing: `zero-test`, `field-id-hint`. The order and bootstrap seed is the acceptance interval's `seed`: SplitMix64 over the campaign seed XOR the first eight bytes of the SHA-256 of the cell key seeds the generator that counterbalances the pair order and draws the bootstrap resamples. The generator is xoshiro256**, pinned by the `abtest.rs` digest each receipt heading names. Its derivation is `dev/tools/tuning-campaign-support/src/abtest.rs`.

What a receipt's conversion probes time: `setup` of every workload times one-shot fixture or code construction; `pack` of `bch-encode-batch`, `bch-encode-batch-alloc`, `bch-encode-batch-parallel` times one `encode_workspace()` construction, the caller-side preparation the route borrows, because the families pack messages inside the timed call; `batch_fill` of `bch-encode-batch`, `bch-encode-batch-alloc`, `bch-encode-batch-parallel`, `dvb-bch-encode` times regeneration of the batch with the fixture generator, a harness cost a consumer replaces with a copy of its own data; `dispatch` of `bch-encode-batch`, `bch-encode-batch-alloc` times the family selection `encode_batch_into` performs per call; `unpack` of `ldpc-syndrome`, `ldpc-codeword-check` times allocating a `BitVec` with one bit of capacity per check and appending one bit per check, the output construction of the CSR matvec; `unpack` of `dense-matvec` times allocating the output vector and appending one bit per row; `dispatch` of `dense-rref`, `dense-matvec`, `dense-transpose` times the route selection the consumer performs per call.

# Immutable superseded LDPC evidence

> **Diátaxis Type:** Reference

The protocol-v1 campaigns and preparation records imported from salvage commit
`02beeb9c` remain immutable superseded evidence. Their exact original bytes and
paths are indexed by [superseded-v1-evidence.json](superseded-v1-evidence.json).
Their historical acceptance summaries describe protocol v1 and do not establish
protocol-v3 acceptance or supply resolution for v3 confirmation. Rejected,
interrupted, method-invalid and losing outcomes remain visible in those records.
Current protocol-v3 families use fresh pilots, ledgers and confirmations.
